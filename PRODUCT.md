# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indie game developers, 3D artists, and Blender learners — mostly self-taught creators looking to level up from YouTube tutorials to a structured, professional workflow. They use this at their desk, often late at night, focused and motivated but frustrated by scattered free content.

## Product Purpose

Potter Academy sells premium Blender courses (low-poly character creation, rigging, animation) through high-conversion landing pages. The design must convey technical credibility, creative energy, and premium quality without feeling corporate or generic.

## Brand Personality

Bold, technical, creative. The aesthetic says "I know what I'm doing and I'll teach you properly." Not flashy startup energy — more like a skilled mentor with taste.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (white backgrounds, blue CTAs, hero-metric templates)
- Udemy/Skillshare marketplace feel (crowded, low trust, discount-countdown urgency)
- Overly playful/childish edtech (bright primaries, cartoon illustrations)
- Corporate training portals (sterile, blue-navy, stock photography)

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work** — real product screenshots and video, not abstract illustrations
2. **Confidence without shouting** — bold typography and dark surfaces, not neon overload
3. **One clear path** — every page drives to a single enrollment action
4. **Creator-to-creator** — the tone is peer mentorship, not institutional authority

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast for all body text
- Reduced motion preferences respected
- Keyboard-navigable interactive elements
